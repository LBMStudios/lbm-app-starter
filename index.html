About / LBM Studios

Lucas Beathyate is a multidisciplinary creative practitioner based in Montevideo. His work moves across visual arts, commercial photography, creative direction, product, software and applied AI.

LBM Studios operates as an independent practice built around one principle: use the medium the idea actually needs.
